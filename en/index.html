04 — ME

About me

Hi, I'm Ziya Azizov, known online as ayfar.

I was born in Baku in 2005 and still live there. I'm an engineer working in programming, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence.

I earned my bachelor's degree in Mechatronics and Robotics at Azerbaijan State Oil and Industry University (ASOIU). I'm now studying for a master's degree in control in technical systems at Baku Higher Oil School (BHOS).

This world was opened to me by the Fundamentals of Programming course in my first year. In 2023, in my second year, I got seriously into programming and cybersecurity and started on my own by installing Kali Linux on my computer.

I believe cybersecurity and AI will stay among the strongest fields for many years, and skilled engineers will always be needed there. Alongside that work, I build projects that can make life noticeably easier for companies, professors and everyday people. All of them are on this site.
